Digital Twin

PV tilt scans in 3D.

LiDAR rover scans of the testbed, leveled to the local ground, with the fitted plane and measured tilt of each panel frame. Switch between the baseline and the two tilt scenarios to see the reconfigured frame move.

Interactive Viewer

Orbit, pan and zoom the point cloud.

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. Use Profile, Unit B to look along the panel edge and see its slope against the ground. Colour by plane fit to check how flat each fitted surface is. The viewer may take a few seconds to load (about 13 MB of point data).
