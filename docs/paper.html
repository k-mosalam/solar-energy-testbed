Background Paper

Conference paper describing the rover-based monitoring framework.

This page isolates the research paper context from the operational and data pages.

Why this paper matters

The paper motivates spatial and geometric monitoring of solar farms because some early-stage anomalies may not appear immediately in electrical performance data.

What it covers

  • Rover architecture and deployment logic.
  • LiDAR-based mapping and SLAM workflow.
  • Articulated sensing motivation.
  • Portable PV testbed concept.
  • Validation path from laboratory to field.

A LiDAR-Enabled Ground Rover Framework for Smart Monitoring in Utility-Scale Solar Power Systems: Design and Preliminary Validation frames the robotic monitoring layer that now sits alongside the electrical and environmental parts of the site.
