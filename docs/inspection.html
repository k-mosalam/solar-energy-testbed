Robotic Inspection

LiDAR-enabled rover for spatial monitoring of solar infrastructure.

This page keeps all rover, LiDAR, SLAM, point-cloud, and geometric-inspection material together.

Ground mobility

A four-wheel-drive unmanned ground vehicle moves through array rows for close-range inspection.

LiDAR and SLAM

LiDAR sensing and SLAM-based mapping produce point clouds for repeatable geometry checks.

Articulated sensing

An articulated sensor mount improves viewpoint flexibility and reduces occlusion.

Inspection Targets

What the rover can help detect

  • Panel displacement, tilt change, or misalignment.
  • Frame deformation and support movement after wind or impact events.
  • Vegetation encroachment and obstruction near panel rows.
  • Terrain settlement and clearance reduction.
  • Repeatable geometric change between inspection cycles.
Workflow

From scanning to decision support

  • Navigate to predefined waypoints.
  • Acquire LiDAR scans from multiple viewpoints.
  • Register scans into a point cloud.
  • Compare repeated scans to identify changes.
  • Integrate spatial observations with inverter and environmental data.
