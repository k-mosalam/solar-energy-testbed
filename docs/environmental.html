Environmental Impacts

Controlled studies of realistic solar-farm stressors.

This page groups the stressors and resilience themes that drive the electrical and structural monitoring program.

Soiling and cleaning

Dust accumulation, coating durability, and response to air, water, or pulsed-jet cleaning systems.

Thermal effects

Temperature-driven performance variation, thermal cycling, and temperature-related degradation patterns.

Shading and vegetation

Vegetation encroachment, surface obstruction, and partial shading effects on string-level performance.

Wind and vibration

Frame movement, panel displacement, connection loosening, and dynamic response under excitation.

Hail and impact

Localized impact scenarios to evaluate geometric change, structural vulnerability, and recovery indicators.

Smoke, ash, and heat

Particulate exposure and controlled heat-flux scenarios, subject to safety review and approval.

Robotics Integration

LiDAR-enabled rover inspection

A ground rover equipped with LiDAR and autonomous navigation supports repeated geometric inspection and change detection over time.

Research Outcomes

Data for resilient solar infrastructure

The testbed is intended to produce field-oriented datasets for O&M, reliability, digital twins, and resilience-centered planning.
