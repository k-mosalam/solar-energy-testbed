Research Vision

Linking electrical performance, physical condition, and field exposure.

The platform supports repeatable experiments on photovoltaic asset health, structural behavior, and environmental stress. It is meant to connect what the inverter reports, what the hardware is physically experiencing, and what autonomous inspection can measure over time.

Electrical

String-level inverter measurements for power, energy, and operating state.

Structural

Frame drift, support movement, vibration, impact response, and geometry change.

Digital Twin

Field-informed datasets for resilience, O&M, and condition-aware models.

Current Status

Under construction and moving toward deployment

The platform is moving toward indoor and outdoor studies, with inverter integration, initial logging, and robotic inspection trials forming the near-term priorities.
