Physical Platform

Portable PV testbed with high-voltage string inverter architecture.

The physical platform page collects the hardware configuration, deployment setting, and current assembly direction without mixing in energy charts or robotics detail.

Platform Summary

Configured for flexible PV experiments.

The testbed uses utility-scale thin-film CdTe modules and an inverter architecture aligned with their high-voltage, low-current behavior. The design is meant to stay flexible as the research program expands.
